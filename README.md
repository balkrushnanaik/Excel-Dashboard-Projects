## 📊 Types of Dashboards

### 💰 Sales Dashboard
Analyze sales performance across products, regions, sales representatives, and time periods.

**Possible KPIs:**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Sales Growth

### 👥 Customer Dashboard
Analyze customer behavior, customer segments, purchasing patterns, and revenue contribution.

### 👨‍💼 Employee Dashboard
Analyze employee performance, salaries, departments, attendance, and other HR metrics.

### 💵 Financial Dashboard
Analyze revenue, expenses, profit, budget vs actuals, and financial trends.

### 🛒 E-Commerce Dashboard
Analyze orders, products, customers, revenue, categories, and sales channels.

### 📢 Marketing Dashboard
Analyze campaigns, leads, conversions, customer acquisition, and marketing performance.

---

## 📈 Example Business Insights

The dashboards are designed to answer questions such as:

- Which product generates the highest revenue?
- Which region has the highest sales?
- What is the monthly sales trend?
- Which sales representative performs best?
- Which products have declining sales?
- What percentage of total revenue comes from each category?
- What is the average order value?
- Which customer segment generates the most revenue?
- How does actual performance compare with the target?
- Which business areas require further attention?
